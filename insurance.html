Common Questions

Answers to What You're Wondering

Still have questions? I'm always happy to talk — no obligation, no pressure.

How much does life insurance actually cost?
+

It depends on your age, health, and the coverage amount you choose. Many healthy individuals can get strong term coverage for under 0–0 a month. The earlier you lock in rates, the better — and I'll shop multiple carriers to find you the best option.

What's the difference between term and whole life insurance?
+

Term insurance covers you for a set period (10, 20, 30 years) and is the most affordable pure protection. Whole life covers you for life and builds cash value over time. The right choice depends entirely on your goals — and I'll help you figure that out.

What is an IUL and is it right for me?
+

An Indexed Universal Life (IUL) is a permanent life insurance policy where the cash value is tied to a stock market index — giving you upside potential with downside protection. It's a powerful tool for tax-free retirement income. Whether it's right for you depends on your financial situation and goals, which we'll explore together.

Do I need life insurance if I'm young and healthy?
+

Being young and healthy is actually the best time to get covered — you'll lock in the lowest possible rates before health changes affect your premiums. Many of my best strategies are built for exactly this situation.

What is mortgage protection insurance?
+

Mortgage protection is a life insurance policy specifically designed to pay off or continue paying your mortgage if you pass away unexpectedly. It gives your family the ability to stay in their home without financial stress during an already difficult time.

Is there any obligation when I request a quote?
+

Absolutely none. A quote is just information — it helps you understand your options. I'll never pressure you to make a decision, and I'll make sure you feel informed and confident whatever you choose.
